## Manifesto of the Civic Alliance for Streets Without Dog Shit

We, the citizens who dare to dream of sidewalks free from unexpected biological surprises, unite under a simple and noble cause: **to reclaim our public spaces from the tyranny of unattended dog poop**.

We believe in a city where stepping outside does not require the reflexes of a parkour athlete. A city where the soles of our shoes are respected, not assaulted. A city where responsibility triumphs over the seductive temptation of “I’ll pick it up later” (you won’t).

Our demands are modest:

1. **Pick up your dog’s shit.**  
   Yes, even when no one is watching. Especially then.

2. **Carry bags at all times.**  
   You are not a revolutionary for doing so; you are simply an adult.

3. **Acknowledge that your dog is cute, but its poop is not.**  
   Beauty is subjective. The smell is not.

We call on all fellow citizens—dog owners, non-owners, and victims of unfortunate sidewalk incidents—to join our peaceful movement. Let us create a future where kids run freely, where shoes stay clean, and where no one must perform that awkward grass-scraping dance ever again.

This is our vision. This is our stand.  
**Together, we can build a better city—one bagged turd at a time.**
